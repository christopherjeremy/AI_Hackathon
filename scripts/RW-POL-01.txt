Rental Agreement & Terms
Document RW-POL-01 · Version 3.0 · Effective 1 January 2025 · Applies to all rentals
This agreement sets out the terms on which RideWise Mobility Pvt. Ltd. ("RideWise") rents bikes,
scooters and electric vehicles to customers. By confirming a booking in the RideWise app, the
customer accepts these terms, together with the Cancellation & Refund Policy, the Late Return &
Damage Penalty Schedule and the EV User Guide.
1. Eligibility
1.1 The customer must be at least 18 years old and hold a valid driving licence for the class of
vehicle booked.
1.2 Each customer may hold one RideWise account, registered with a mobile number and email
address.
2. KYC verification
2.1 Every account goes through KYC (Know Your Customer) verification of the driving licence and
identity. An account has one of three KYC statuses:
KYC status
What the customer can do
VERIFIED
Book and pick up any vehicle.
PENDING
Book in advance, but the vehicle is released only after KYC is verified.
REJECTED
Cannot rent. Any existing booking is cancelled by RideWise.
2.2 If KYC is still pending at the scheduled pickup time, or is rejected, RideWise cancels the booking.
A booking cancelled by RideWise is always refunded in full (see the Cancellation & Refund Policy).
3. Hubs, pickup and return
3.1 Vehicles are picked up from and returned to the same RideWise hub. RideWise operates seven
hubs, all in Bengaluru: Koramangala, Indiranagar, Jayanagar, HSR Layout, Electronic City, Whitefield
and Hebbal.
3.2 The rental period runs from the scheduled pickup time to the scheduled return time shown in the
booking. Rentals are booked in whole hours, with a minimum of 2 hours.
3.3 A return after the scheduled return time is a late return and is charged under the Late Return &
Damage Penalty Schedule.
4. Rental charges
4.1 Each vehicle has an hourly rate and a daily rate (per 24 hours). The rental charge is always the
cheaper combination for the booked duration:
• every full 24-hour block is charged at the daily rate; and
• the remaining hours are charged at the hourly rate, but never more than one daily rate.

4.3 GST at 18% is added to rental charges, penalties and fees. When a refund is issued, the GST on
the refunded amount is refunded in the same proportion.
4.4 The full rental charge is paid when the booking is made. Payment may be completed in more
than one instalment before pickup. A failed payment attempt is not a payment and is never charged.
5. Security deposit

5.2 The deposit is not a rental charge, carries no GST, and is returned to the original payment
method within 3 working days of the vehicle's return. Unpaid penalties may be recovered from it.
5.3 Deposit returns are processed directly by the payment gateway and are not shown in RideWise
booking or payment records.
6. Customer tiers
6.1 Customers are placed in one of three tiers (STANDARD, SILVER or GOLD) based on their rental
history. Tier benefits, including any grace period on late returns, are set out in Annexure A of the
Late Return & Damage Penalty Schedule.
7. No-shows
7.1 A booking is a no-show when the customer neither picks up the vehicle within 60 minutes of the
scheduled pickup time nor cancels the booking before the scheduled pickup time.
7.2 The rental charge for a no-show is not refunded. No additional fee is charged for a no-show.
8. Customer responsibilities
8.1 The customer must ride safely, follow traffic laws, wear a helmet and not lend the vehicle to
anyone else.
8.2 The customer is responsible for traffic fines incurred during the rental period.
8.3 Damage found at return is assessed by hub staff and charged under the Late Return & Damage
Penalty Schedule.
