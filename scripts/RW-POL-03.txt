Cancellation & Refund Policy
Document RW-POL-03 · Version 2 · Effective 1 July 2026, 00:00 IST
Scope. This version replaces Version 1 for all bookings made on or after 1 July 2026, 00:00 IST.
Bookings made before that time continue to follow Version 1 (RW-POL-02), even if they are cancelled
after 1 July 2026.
Why it changed. Version 2 introduces a wider full-refund window and a new partial-refund slab, so
that customers who cancel closer to pickup still receive part of their rental charge back.
1. Cancellation by the customer
1.1 The refund depends on how long before the scheduled pickup time the booking is cancelled. The
approved refund schedule is reproduced in Annexure 1 below.
1.2 The refund percentage is applied to the rental charge actually paid. GST on the refunded amount
is refunded in the same proportion.
2. Cancellation by RideWise
2.1 Unchanged from Version 1: a booking cancelled by RideWise is always refunded 100%.
3. No-shows
3.1 Unchanged from Version 1: no refund is given for a no-show.
4. Refund processing
4.1 Refunds are initiated within 3 working days of the cancellation and credited to the original
payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Security deposits are returned in full on cancellation.
