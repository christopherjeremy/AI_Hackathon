2. Before you ride
2.1 Check the battery level on the dashboard at pickup. Every EV leaves the hub with at least 80%
charge.
2.2 Plan your trip so that you return with enough charge. Riding in Eco mode gives the longest
range.
3. Charging
3.1 You may charge at any RideWise hub or an approved public charger. Charging at a RideWise hub
is free.

5. Frequently asked questions
Q. What happens if the battery runs out during my trip?
Stop in a safe place and call RideWise Support on +91 80 4000 1234. A team from the nearest hub
will bring a charged vehicle or tow the EV. Towing is free if the vehicle had a fault; otherwise a
recovery charge may apply.
Q. Can I charge the EV at home?
Yes. Use only the RideWise charging cable supplied under the seat, with a properly earthed 15 A
socket.
Q. What if I return the EV with less than 10% battery?
A flat low-battery charging fee applies, as set out in the Late Return & Damage Penalty Schedule.
Q. Does a low-battery return also count as a late return?
No. They are separate. If the EV is both late and below 10%, both fees apply.
Q. Who pays for damage to the charging port or cable?
Damage is assessed by hub staff and handled under the damage section of the Penalty Schedule.
Q. My dashboard shows a battery warning light. What should I do?
Stop riding, switch off the vehicle and call RideWise Support. Do not try to open the battery
compartment.
