Cancellation & Refund Policy
Document RW-POL-02 · Version 1 · Effective 1 January 2025
Scope. This version applies to bookings made before 1 July 2026, 00:00 IST. Bookings made on
or after that time follow Version 2 of this policy (RW-POL-03). The version is decided by when the
booking was made, not by when it was cancelled or when the rental starts.
1. Cancellation by the customer
1.1 The customer can cancel a booking in the RideWise app at any time before the scheduled pickup
time. The refund depends on how long before the scheduled pickup the cancellation is made:
Time between cancellation and scheduled pickup
Refund of rental charge
24 hours or more
100%
6 hours or more, but less than 24 hours
50%
Less than 6 hours
No refund (0%)
1.2 The refund percentage is applied to the rental charge actually paid. GST on the refunded amount
is refunded in the same proportion.
2. Cancellation by RideWise
2.1 If RideWise cancels a booking for any reason (for example vehicle unavailability or KYC not
verified), the customer receives a 100% refund, whatever the timing.
3. No-shows
3.1 A no-show (see Rental Agreement clause 7) is not a cancellation. No refund is given for a
no-show.
4. Refund processing
4.1 Refunds are initiated within 3 working days of the cancellation and credited to the original
payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
4.2 Security deposits are returned in full on cancellation and are not subject to this refund schedule.
5. Example
Example
Booking made
10 May 2026, 18:00 IST (Version 1 applies)
Scheduled pickup
20 May 2026, 09:00 IST
Cancelled
19 May 2026, 21:00 IST, which is 12 hours before pickup

₹500
Refund
50% of ₹500 = ₹250 (plus GST of ₹45 refunded)
