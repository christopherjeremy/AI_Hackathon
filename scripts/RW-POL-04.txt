Late Return & Damage Penalty Schedule
Document RW-POL-04 · Version 2.1 · Effective 1 January 2025
This schedule sets out the charges for late returns, low battery on EVs, and damage. All penalties
attract GST at 18%.
1. Late return fee
1.1 Lateness is measured from the scheduled return time to the actual return time.
1.2 Each late hour, or part of an hour, is charged at 1.5 times the vehicle's hourly rate. For
example, 20 minutes late counts as 1 hour; 1 hour 10 minutes late counts as 2 hours.
1.3 Customers in some tiers receive a grace period before a late fee applies. Grace periods are listed
in Annexure A. If a return is later than the grace period, the grace period does not reduce the
lateness: the full lateness is charged as in clause 1.2.
